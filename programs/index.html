Programs and Events

Fiscal Year 2026–2027

43 programs and events across nine areas, from your first ribbon cutting to leading the business community. Find what fits your business today.

Signature Events

4

Longstanding gatherings that connect the business community and celebrate McAllen.

  • Good Day McAllen Luncheon

    Quarterly

    The Chamber welcomes its newest members, shares updates and gives members a room to promote their business.

  • Man and Woman of the Year Awards

    Annual

    A formal gala honoring two people for their service to McAllen. Proceeds support Chamber programs.

  • Mayor's Cup Golf Tournament

    Annual

    An 18-hole, three-player scramble with an awards dinner. A relaxed way to build business relationships.

  • Christmas in the Park

    Annual

    A two-day family celebration that opens the McAllen Holiday Parade, with vendors, an artisan market and live entertainment.

Small Business and Entrepreneurship

14

Programs that help founders start, learn and grow.

  • 1 Million Cups

    Monthly

    A founder presents their business and the room asks how it can help. Free to attend.

  • Latina Hope

    Monthly

    Workshops, hands-on skills and a pitch for seed money for women with small businesses.

  • Innovation Texas

    5 cohorts a year

    An accelerator of eight weekly sessions with instruction, mentoring and business development support.

  • Demo Day

    Annual

    A bilingual pitch competition and showcase for Innovation Texas founders.

  • Government Contracting Academy

    Annual

    A training week on how to sell to government: registration, proposals and purchasing.

  • Global Entrepreneurship Week

    Annual

    A week of events with a Resource Expo that puts Valley partners in one room.

  • How to Break a Business

    Annual

    Local owners share their setbacks and what they learned.

  • Business Consulting

    Ongoing

    One-on-one guidance on planning, market strategy and next steps.

  • Complimentary Professional Services

    Ongoing

    30-minute consultations with member professionals in legal, accounting, banking, insurance, HR and real estate.

  • SATOP

    Ongoing

    Up to 40 free hours of engineering help for Texas small businesses.

  • SizeUp

    Ongoing

    Market research on competitors, customers and locations.

  • Virtual Offices

    Ongoing

    A business address, local phone line and mail service at the McAllen Creative Incubator.

  • Certificates of Origin

    Ongoing

    Trade documents that verify where exported goods were made.

  • Economic Pulse Report

    Monthly

    Regional economic data and the trends shaping local business.
